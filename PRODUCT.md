# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ensō Time is for independent professionals, operators, creators, and knowledge workers who spend most of their working day across desktop applications. They use it privately on their own computer to understand where time went, recover missing context, and decide which recurring task is worth automating first.

The primary workflow is a quiet daily loop: leave tracking running, answer only the ambiguous check-ins, review the day, and use the 7/14/28-day diagnosis to choose an automation candidate.

## Product Purpose

Ensō Time turns local activity data into a useful personal diagnosis without sending work history to a server. Success means a user can install one lightweight application, get an accurate timeline with minimal interruption, understand patterns against their own baseline, and copy a privacy-safe prompt that helps ChatGPT or Claude recommend the first automation to build.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, technical, and quietly premium. The product should feel disciplined and calm rather than gamified or judgmental. Its voice is concise, observant, and practical: it reports evidence, asks for missing context, and recommends a next move without moralizing.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI productivity dashboards filled with gradients, glowing cards, and inflated scores.
- Surveillance software that hides what it records or makes users feel watched.
- Gamified habit trackers that use streak anxiety, guilt, trophies, or celebratory noise.
- Dense enterprise analytics that require configuration before they become useful.
- ActivityWatch's full technical dashboard as the primary user experience; Ensō Time uses its capture engine but presents a narrower diagnostic workflow.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence before advice.** Every recommendation must show the local behavior that produced it.
- **Interruption must be earned.** Ask only when an interval is genuinely ambiguous, and preserve unanswered questions without repeating them.
- **Private by construction.** Raw application and window-title data stays on the device; outbound actions are explicit and previewable.
- **One clear next move.** Each screen should make the most useful action obvious without turning the interface into a collection of cards.
- **Calm accountability.** Describe patterns against the user's own baseline and goals, never against a universal definition of productivity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, names, roles, and error messaging. All essential meaning must survive without color. Motion is limited to state feedback, honors `prefers-reduced-motion`, and never gates access to content. Spanish (LatAm) and English receive equal layout and copy support, including longer translated strings and locale-aware dates and durations.
